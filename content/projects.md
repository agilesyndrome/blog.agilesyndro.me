### TTCStatus.ca

[TTCStatus.ca](https://ttcstatus.ca) — a live map of Toronto's streetcar network, with real-time TTC vehicle positions.

**Why I built it:** I live here. I ride these things. And I've spent far too many winter evenings at a stop
watching the display insist my streetcar was "due" while it was, demonstrably, nowhere. So I built the tool
I wanted to exist: every route on a map, every vehicle in real time. The user I design for is me, standing
in the cold, deciding whether to wait or walk.

### NO SECURITY

[NO SECURITY](https://nosecurity.dev) — a cybersecurity museum of mistakes.

**Why I built it:** Every post-mortem I've ever read sanitises the best part: the human who did something
completely reasonable that still ended in catastrophe. After 17 years in security, my favourite stories were
always told quietly, by people asking me not to use their name. So I'm building the museum those stories
deserve — anonymous, fictionalised exhibits where the names and lines of code have been changed to protect
what's left of the guilty. Submissions are accepted; identities are not. No cookies, no analytics, no
tracking: privacy is part of the exhibition, and the doors open once no single story can be traced back to
the person who submitted it.

### Command Line Tools

[TOTP](https://gitlab.com/agilesyndrome/totp)

**Why I built it:** My automated test suites needed 2FA codes, and my phone was tired of being interrogated
every five minutes. So I taught myself Go by building exactly the thing I needed — my first Go project, so
